<Warning>
This feature is **deprecated**.

Chapterization is replaced by [Audio-to-LLM](/chapters/audio-intelligence/audio-to-llm), which is more powerful and fully customizable: you write your own prompts to segment long audio, generate headlines and summaries, or structure the transcript however your product needs.
</Warning>

Existing `chapterization` requests continue to work for backward compatibility, but new integrations should use Audio-to-LLM instead.
